## Facts that only appear inside images

Put these into the page text during the rewrite. Search engines and AI tools can't read text inside images.

- **oceanx:** dives to 248 m and 1,000 m; 86% species-identification accuracy across 100,000 possible species; sensors and Schilling manipulator arms are labelled.
- **oceanx-digital-twin:** CTD casts in 3D (840 m depth, dissolved-oxygen readings); a coral identified by AI (*Acanella arbuscula*, about 599 m); Timor-Leste is the location shown.
- **raf-museum:** nine projectors; supported by Northrop Grumman; the debate motion is about robots making battlefield decisions; a live vote result (89%) and a countdown timer.
- **lego-playtables:** the venue photo looks like a LEGOLAND Discovery Center in China, but the text never says where the sites are.
- **co-cog:**
  - three cognitive tasks: picture recognition, recall of a 12-word list, and a 3×3 pattern sequence;
  - a VAS wellbeing scale with faces;
  - a safety screen pointing users to 111 and 999;
  - read-aloud audio on every screen and anonymous login codes;
  - a results dashboard across 12 categories (sleep, falls, pain and others);
  - five visual styles compared in Figma.
- **co-play:** a webcam-only kite exergame (no wearables), with one- or two-player modes, timed rounds, and stars to collect (for example 24). The page currently has no text to put this in.
- **tale-together:** the game is "FlashStory Crafter"; each player has 60 seconds per turn and passes a virtual hourglass; players title the story at the end.
- **bosco-dental-studio:** a Windows receptionist tool that outputs length, urgency, expectations and likely outcome; it records the tooth by quadrant and gives timings per clinician level.
- **breez-dental:**
  - structured notes (BPE scores, recall interval, risk classification);
  - three appointment tabs running in parallel;
  - in-app teaching of dental terms;
  - prompt management and model tracking.
- **mere-mortals:** hundreds of enemies on screen; seven named weapons; a custom Unity weapon editor (Swipe has 8 levels, with damage from 10 to 40).
- **the-tarot-experience:** the award was in 2025; the feedback form rated "How meaningful was the experience?" on a scale of 1 to 10.
- **the-tarot-experience-web-reading:** GPT-4 Turbo, with the reading streamed live, an emailed reading and shareable card images.
- **the-north-pole-escape:** Unity WebGL; 9 rooms plus tutorials; puzzles not mentioned in the text.
- **lovemore:** 8 map locations and 5 relationship stats; "Rated one of the best dating sims of all time".
- **friendly-fox-adventures:**
  - the trail sign credits Develop Outdoors, Sean Longcroft, Sussex Community Foundation and Telscombe Town Council;
  - collect 8 potion ingredients;
  - QR code for downloading the app.
- **seaweeds-scrambled-spell:** collect 5 letters; three minigames (cobwebs, bug squashing, riddles in sets of 3).
- **popup-view:** campaigns are named in the app; the butterfly campaign is NFT art sold through KnownOrigin. The text never mentions NFTs.
- **abstract-painters:** HTC Vive Pro; Pure Data and MIDI; work dated March 2019.
- **visual-effects:** real-time Unity renders, though the page never names Unity.
- **isles-of-generation:** biomes are chosen from temperature and moisture.
