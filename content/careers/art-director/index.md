**Location:** Typically 2 days office / rest remote, with room for flexibility

**Position:** 30 hours/week (core preference: 10am–5pm, with flexibility on schedule)

**Experience:** Open to existing art directors or artists ready to promote

### **Our Studio**

Unusual Technologies is a small but growing team of specialists. We help on any project in any industry that needs technology, design, art or a combination.  Museums, theme parks, video games, academia and more. 

We work extensively with freelancers to make sure we assemble the right team for each job, using an in-house specialist to bring it all together. This ad is for an in-house art specialist.

Our visual quality to date has been to a mixed standard. We intend to focus on high value projects moving forward with a visual quality to match it.

### **This Role**

While this is a leadership role in terms of strategy, we are a small, flat team. You won't be managing a large department or an agency; you will be a solo operator working across client projects, in-house projects and helping with marketing materials. You will tackle much of the work yourself but you will also inherit, build and maintain a list of freelancers to help when you are overstretched or a level of specialism is needed that you do not possess. In those cases you will be in charge of managing the external team, conveying the vision and ensuring the necessary quality is met. 

**What you’ll do**

- Lead all art for the company, setting and upholding a consistent high visual standard across every project
- Produce much of the work yourself with tools/AI, including and UI/UX design, 2D art, 3D modelling and texturing, animation, pitch decks, website, etc.
- Decide when to bring in outside help, hire and manage freelancers to cover specialist areas or extra capacity
- Work closely with marketing to prototype new game concepts, test their market viability, and improve marketing materials, store pages etc
- Establish and enforce good processes and pipelines,including project planning, task estimation and prioritisation, and file hygiene 
- Communicate and collaborate closely with the wider team and our clients

**What we’re looking for**

- A strong eye across visual disciplines, both 2D and 3D
- Experience producing art for real-time engines (ideally Unity) and an understanding of their constraints. 
- High standards and a real focus on quality
- The ability to make complex or technical subject matter clear and engaging. 
- Comfort moving between very different project types
- Keenness to use AI tools well, with the judgement to know when another tool or person will do the job better
- Disciplined and process-driven, holding yourself and others to the same standard 
- Keeps up to date with new tools, processes and trends

**Nice to have**

- VFX, sketching and concept art, video editing, technical scripting
