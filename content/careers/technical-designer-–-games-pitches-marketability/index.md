**Compensation:** £30,000 - £40,000

**Location:** 2 office days in Brighton, (room for flexibility)

**Hours:** 30 hours/week. Core preference: 10am–5pm, (room for flexibility)

**Holiday**: Unlimited with a minimum of 25 days plus bank holidays

### **The role**

A many-hats design role in a small studio. You’ll shape ideas into games and client projects, write the pitches that win them, and prototype them with AI, with no coding background needed. Roughly, your time will split between client work and developing and testing our own games.

### **About us**

Unusual Technologies builds bespoke creative and immersive technology for clients such as museums, theme parks, universities and games companies. That client work is our foundation, and it continues alongside our next step: designing and launching our own products, starting with games.

We’re a deliberately small and close-knit team built for the long term.When work peaks, we bring in trusted freelancers for the overspill rather than hiring and letting go, so the core team has real job security.

### What you’ll do

- Write pitches, tenders and grant applications, using our tools to find new opportunities
- Run user test groups, including with young or vulnerable users in health tech
- Help with B2B marketing: case studies, our website and LinkedIn
- Work closely with the wider team and our clients
- Design game concepts and client projects, from first idea to launch and beyond
- Build playable prototypes with AI tools
- Test whether new game concepts have a market, and working on the marketing plans and implementation
- Keep up to date with games, design and technology
- Help keep projects organised: planning, estimating, prioritising and tidy files

### What we’re looking for

- A DBS certificate, or eligibility for one 
- Excellent writing for applications, pitches, etc
- Confidence working with clients and running workshops or user test sessions 
- Examples of games/software you made (vibe coded or other)
- Interest in all areas of game design including market viability 
- Comfort with analytics and exploring novel marketing
- Curiosity and a well-rounded knowledge of the world 

**Nice to have**

- UI/UX examples
- An interest in immersive technology
